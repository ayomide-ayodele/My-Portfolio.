Professional Profile

About Me

I’m Ayomide, a product professional who turns complex user and business problems into practical digital solutions, with an economist’s lens on incentives, trade-offs, and measurable outcomes.

My path into product grew from an Economics background and a curiosity about how people, businesses, and technology interact to create value. Since then, I’ve contributed to 5+ digital product engagements across fintech, healthtech, education, and AI, building experience across discovery, research, strategy, requirements, delivery, and testing. I’ve also conducted 30+ user interviews to validate the problem space and shape Lumit, an AI-powered education product I founded.

Building Lumit has strengthened my approach to product ownership, from validating a problem and defining the right solution to making decisions under resource constraints and moving an idea from validation to a real product. Its selection into the Tony Elumelu Foundation Entrepreneurship Programme has been a meaningful milestone in that journey.

I’m particularly interested in building products at the intersection of financial services and education, where thoughtful product decisions can improve access, experiences, and outcomes at scale.
